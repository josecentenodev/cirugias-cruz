# 0008 — Notifications, reminders, and payments are out of scope

## Status

Established (current iteration) — as a deferral, not a design.

## Decision

Notifications and reminders are explicitly out of scope for the current
iteration. Payment/subscription management for physicians is likewise out
of scope. None of these are to be designed, modeled, or hinted at in the
domain until explicitly requested in a future iteration.

## Rationale

Keeps the current discovery/implementation phase focused on the core
clinical loop (patients → surgeries → controls → research) without
speculative design for concerns that have no current product requirement.

## Not decided here

Everything about these systems — deferred in full.
